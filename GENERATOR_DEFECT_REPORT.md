## Cross-cutting
- The single source of the wrong-gender problem is `getProductUrl()` (defect #3) — used by the `getProductUrl` Handlebars helper (registered `scripts/generate-products.cjs:333`), by `buildCollectionSchema` (#3 ItemList), and by product schema `@id`/`url` (product pages themselves are correct because they use `getProductUrlForHierarchy`, `:430`). Breadcrumbs, sitemap, and product-page canonicals already use the hierarchy-aware builder (`:85-91`).
- The reviews mismatch (#4) is a single line at `generate-products.cjs:441`; the correct data already flows through `reviewSystem.getProductReviews(id, { verifiedOnly:false })` at `:205`/`:574`.
- No fix in this plan edits `dist/`; every defect above has a generator/template/data-file home.
